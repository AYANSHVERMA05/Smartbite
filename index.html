Innovative Engineering

6 Pillars of the SmartBite System

Built ground-up with custom algorithms rather than superficial UI clones.

Smart Budget Meal Planner

Takes any daily budget (₹200, ₹350, ₹500) and executes combinatorial heuristics to partition optimal Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner triples with protein density prioritization.

AI Food Recommendations

Content-based filtering using TfidfVectorizer and Cosine Similarity. Cold-start fallback dynamically targets dietary tags and ratings.

Surplus Food Reduction

Kitchen owners list fresh surplus cafeteria batches at 40–60% flash discounts before strict pickup deadlines, curbing university food waste.

Hostel Group Orders

Hostel roommates join via 6-character room codes. Individual orders combine into one master COD checkout with per-student bill breakdown.

Hostel Mess Subscriptions

Weekly (7-day) and monthly (30-day) dining plans delivering meals straight to campus hostel gates, complete with pause and resume controls.

Macro & Nutrition Filter

Real-time calorie thresholds, minimum protein limits, and strict vegetarian safety indicators for fitness and diet-conscious students.

Academic Defense

Technical Viva Questions & Solutions

Answers to frequent professor and examiner questions about SmartBite.

Q1 How does the AI Recommendation Engine work under the hood?

It leverages Content-Based Filtering via Scikit-learn. The engine constructs composite feature vectors from Category, Dietary Profile (Veg/Non-Veg), Price Bracket, and Nutritional Tags. Using TfidfVectorizer and cosine_similarity, it compares prospective dishes with the user’s order vector history. If a user is brand new (Cold Start), it gracefully falls back to popularity-weighted top-rated items matching their diet.

Q2 What is the algorithmic complexity of the Budget Meal Planner?

The planner uses a tailored combinatorial heuristic. It partitions catalog dishes into Breakfast (25%), Lunch (45%), and Dinner (30%) buckets, generates valid triples where Cost(B) + Cost(L) + Cost(D) ≤ Budget, and optimizes selection using a composite objective function prioritizing both budget margin utilization and protein density.

Q3 How does Group Ordering preserve data integrity with concurrent members?

Each group session is assigned a unique 6-character alphanumeric room key. Member additions are stored with foreign keys to both the session and the individual user. The master order conversion runs inside an atomic Django database transaction (transaction.atomic()), ensuring item splits and bill totals never desync.
